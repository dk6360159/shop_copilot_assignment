# Tolerance Specifications

For a standard precision-machined surface, a scratch with measured depth below 0.10 mm is acceptable after visual verification.

A surface scratch between 0.10 mm and 0.30 mm requires rework and a second inspection.

A visible crack is not considered an acceptable cosmetic defect. Cracks require quality-engineering review before disposition.

Dimensional deviations outside the work-order drawing tolerance require measurement with the approved gauge and quality review.
